We started Starter Store to make everyday goods that are simple, useful and built to last.

Every product in our shop is chosen or made by a small team. We work with makers we know by name and keep our range deliberately small, so that each piece earns its place.

## What we care about

- **Quality over quantity.** Fewer, better things.
- **Honest materials.** We tell you what our products are made of and where they come from.
- **Real people.** Questions go to our team, not a bot. [Get in touch](/contact).
